About Quote of the day

Quote of the day shows one short, famous quote for each day, with a new one on every tap. Copy it or share it on X. It is a practice project by Zakria Khan (zakyprojects).

How to use

  1. Open the page to see today's quote.
  2. Tap New quote, or press the Space key, for another one.
  3. Copy the quote or share it on X.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. It is free and needs no account.

Does the quote change every day?

Yes. Everyone sees the same quote on the same day, and New quote gives you more.

Will I see the same quote twice?

Not until you have seen all of them.
